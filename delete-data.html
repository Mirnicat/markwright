Request deletion of your data

Last updated: 9 September 2026

This page explains how to delete data held in connection with the Android app Markwright — Punch List PDF (package com.markwright.app), published by the Markwright developer.

1. Your reports, photos and settings

Markwright has no user account and no server. Everything you create — projects, defects, photos, annotations, signatures, notes, your company logo and any optional GPS stamps — is written only to the app's private storage on your own phone. The developer never receives a copy and therefore cannot delete it for you. You delete it yourself:

  • Delete one defect: open the project and tap the bin icon on the defect card.
  • Delete a whole project: on the project list, tap the three-dot menu on the project row and choose Delete.
  • Delete everything at once: Android Settings → Apps → Markwright → Storage → Clear storage. This erases all projects, photos and settings permanently.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes all of this data from your device.

PDF files that you exported and saved or shared yourself are outside the app. Delete those where you saved them.

2. Subscription data

If you bought a Markwright Pro subscription, two records exist outside your phone:

DataHeld byRetention
Purchase token and an anonymous app-user identifier RevenueCat, the subscription management service used by Markwright Kept while the subscription is active; deleted on request as described below
Your Google Play order and payment record Google Play Held by Google under its own policy; the developer cannot delete it

These records contain no name, no email address, no reports, no photos and no location. Markwright never sees your payment details.

3. How to request deletion — step by step

  1. Send an email to markwright.support@gmail.com.
  2. Put Data deletion request — Markwright in the subject line.
  3. In the message, include your Google Play order number, which appears in the purchase receipt Google emailed you (it looks like GPA.0000-0000-0000-00000). This is the only way to locate the right record, because there is no account.
  4. You will receive a confirmation reply within 7 days, and the subscription record will be deleted within 30 days of the request.

4. What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted on requestKept, and why
The RevenueCat purchase token and anonymous app-user identifier linked to your subscription Google Play's own order and payment record, which Google retains for tax, accounting and refund purposes under its policy. The developer has no ability to delete it.

Deleting the subscription record ends the ability to restore Pro on a new device. If you later reinstall Markwright, the app still works; only the Pro features would need to be purchased again.

5. Questions

Write to markwright.support@gmail.com. See also the Privacy Policy.
