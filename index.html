Markwright

Site defect reports, exported as a stamped PDF.

Markwright is an Android app for contractors, site managers and property inspectors. Photograph a defect, mark it up with your finger, and produce a branded PDF punch list — entirely on your phone.

Nothing leaves your phone unless you send it. There is no account to create, no cloud sync, and no analytics. Your photos, notes and reports stay in the app's private storage until you choose to share a PDF.

What it does

  • Capture defect photos and annotate them — arrows, boxes, circles, highlights, numbered markers and text
  • Record severity, status, trade, location and notes for each item
  • Add your company logo, client details and a signature
  • Export a formatted PDF report and share it however you like
  • Optional GPS stamp on each photo, off by default
  • Works fully offline — basements, new builds, sites with no signal
