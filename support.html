Support

Questions, bug reports and feature requests.

Email markwright.support@gmail.com. Messages are usually answered within two working days.

Before you write

If something is not working, including these three things will usually get it fixed in one reply:

  • Your phone model and Android version
  • The app version, shown at the bottom of Settings
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead

Common questions

Where are my reports stored?

In the app's private storage on your phone. They are not uploaded anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.

I changed phones and my reports are gone.

Reports live only on the device that created them and are deliberately excluded from automatic cloud backup, for privacy reasons. Export the PDFs you want to keep before switching devices.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Capturing, annotating and exporting a PDF all work fully offline. An internet connection is only needed to buy or restore a subscription.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Google Play Store app → your profile picture → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Markwright → Cancel. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for.

I paid but Pro is not showing.

Open Settings in the app and use Restore purchases, making sure you are signed in to Google Play with the account you bought it on. If it still does not appear, email us with the Google Play order number from your receipt.

The GPS stamp is missing on my photos.

It is off by default — switch it on in Settings and allow the location permission. Indoors, the app will refuse a fix that is too imprecise to be meaningful rather than print a coordinate that could be wrong.

Can I request deletion of my data?

There is no account and no server, so the developer holds nothing to delete. Deleting a project in the app, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling removes your data from your device.
