Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Short version: Markwright does not have user accounts, does not collect analytics, does not show ads, and does not send your reports, photos or location anywhere. Everything you create stays in the app's private storage on your device until you decide to share a PDF yourself.

1. Who is responsible

Markwright is published by an individual developer. The developer's legal name and address are shown on the app's Google Play listing, as required by Google. For any privacy question, write to markwright.support@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores on your device

All of the following is written to the app's private storage area, which other apps cannot read:

DataWhy
Photos you take or selectThey are the evidence in your report
Your annotations and markupTo render the marked-up image into the PDF
Project, client, inspector name, site address, notesThey appear on the report you produce
Signatures you drawTo sign off a report
Your company logo, if you add oneReport branding
GPS coordinates and accuracy, only if you turn this onTo stamp where a photo was taken
App settings and languageTo remember your preferences

None of this is transmitted to the developer or to any third party by the app.

3. What the app does not do

  • No user account, no sign-in, no email collection
  • No analytics, usage tracking, behavioural profiling or advertising SDKs
  • No advertising of any kind
  • No cloud backup or synchronisation of your reports
  • No selling or sharing of personal data — there is nothing collected to sell

4. The only times the app uses the network

Subscription purchases

If you buy a Markwright Pro subscription, the purchase is processed by Google Play Billing and validated through RevenueCat, a subscription management service. These services receive a purchase token and an anonymous app-user identifier so that your subscription can be recognised and restored. They do not receive your reports, photos, notes or location. Google Play handles the payment itself; the developer never sees your card details.

  • Google Play: policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy

If you never make a purchase, the app makes no network requests at all.

Voice dictation, if you use it

Markwright can transcribe spoken notes. By default it uses your device's on-device speech recognition, and nothing is transmitted. Your device or operating system may offer cloud-based recognition instead; if you enable that in your system settings, your speech is processed by your device maker's service (for example Google) under their privacy policy, not the developer's.

5. Location

The GPS stamp is off by default. You must switch it on in Settings and grant the Android location permission. When enabled, the app records the coordinates, the accuracy figure reported by the device, and the time — and only for photos you capture while it is on. The accuracy figure is always printed alongside the coordinates so a report never looks more precise than it is.

Location data is stored on your device with the report. It leaves your device only inside a PDF that you choose to export and send. The app does no reverse geocoding, no background location, and no location history.

6. Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhat it is used for
CameraTo photograph defects

You can refuse or later revoke any of these in Android Settings. The app keeps working; the corresponding feature simply becomes unavailable.

7. Children

Markwright is a professional tool for construction and property work. It is not directed at children and is not designed for use by them.

8. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it. You are in control:

  • Delete a single defect or a whole project inside the app
  • Clear the app's data in Android Settings → Apps → Markwright → Storage
  • Uninstall the app — this removes all reports, photos and settings from your device

Because there is no account and no server, the developer holds no copy of your data and therefore has nothing to delete on your behalf. If you have questions about deletion, write to markwright.support@gmail.com.

Subscription records held by Google Play and RevenueCat are governed by their own policies and retention rules. You can manage or cancel a subscription in the Google Play Store app at any time.

9. Backups and PDFs you create

The app is configured so that its private data is not included in Android's automatic cloud backup. However, once you export a PDF and save or send it, that file is yours to manage — if you place it in a cloud drive or email it, it is covered by that service's policy, not this one.

10. Diagnostics

The app contains no crash-reporting or telemetry SDK. Android and Google Play may collect crash diagnostics at the operating-system level, according to the settings on your own device; that is outside the app's control and is described in Google's privacy policy.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal data held about you. In Markwright's case, that data is on your own device and under your direct control. If you believe the developer holds personal data about you, write to markwright.support@gmail.com and it will be addressed.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
